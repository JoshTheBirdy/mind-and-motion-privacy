# Mind & Motion: Habits & Focus — Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Early Bird Dev
**Contact:** earlybirdapps.dev@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 2 November 2026

## The short version

Mind & Motion does not collect your data. There is no account to make, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you write or track is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Everything stays on your phone, or in the folder you choose.

## What the app stores, and where

Mind & Motion keeps your habits, tasks, reminders, focus and meditation sessions, journal entries, tags and settings on your device only. It stores them in two places:

- **A database inside the app's private storage** on your phone.
- **Plain text (markdown) files in a "data folder".** You choose where that folder lives when you first open the app: either the app's private storage, or a folder you pick on your phone. The app writes your journal and a backup file there so the data is yours to open, back up or move.

The developer has no access to either place.

## Network use

The app has no server, no sign-in and no online features. It does not send your data over the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software from any third party.

Android lists an "internet" permission for the app because it is a standard part of the framework the app is built on. The app does not use it to send or receive your information.

## Syncing and backups are in your hands

If you keep your data folder somewhere that another app synchronises, such as a cloud-drive app or a file-sync tool, that other app's own privacy policy applies to those files. Mind & Motion has no control over it and does not know about it. You can stop any time by moving the folder back to private storage in Settings.

## Permissions, and what each one is for

The app asks for only what its features need. None of these send information anywhere.

- **Notifications** — to show reminders, timer notifications and the "focus sounds" controls.
- **Exact alarms and full-screen alerts** — so the focus timer and meditation bell go off at the right moment, and so the "stop" screen can appear when the phone is locked.
- **Foreground service (alarm, media playback)** — to keep the timer alarm and your chosen focus sounds playing while the screen is off.
- **Vibration and wake lock** — for the short buzz on a timer or breathing cue, and to keep a timer reliable.
- **Receive boot completed** — so your reminders are set up again after the phone restarts.
- **Calendar (optional)** — only if you choose to show or add events from your phone's calendar in the app. Calendar information is read on the device and is not sent anywhere. You can say no and use the rest of the app.
- **A folder you pick** — if you choose your own data folder, Android's file picker gives the app access to that one folder only.
- **Biometrics or device PIN (optional)** — if you turn on App Lock, the app asks Android to check your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern. The app never sees or stores them. While App Lock is on, screenshots of the app are blocked and the app is hidden in the recent-apps list.

## Home-screen widgets

Widgets show information from the app on your home screen. That information stays on your device.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the database and the private-storage files. If you chose your own data folder, those files stay in that folder until you delete them yourself.

## Children

Mind & Motion is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that sends data off the device, this page will be updated first, and the effective date above will change.
